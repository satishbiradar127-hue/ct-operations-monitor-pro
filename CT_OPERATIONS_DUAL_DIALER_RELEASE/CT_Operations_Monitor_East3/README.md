## Overview
CT Operations Monitor Pro — East-3 Edition provides real-time, non-invasive operational intelligence for CallTools East-3.

### Key Capabilities
- **Two-Column Layout Balance:** Outbound Live Snapshot & Campaigns on LEFT (50%), Inbound Live Queues on RIGHT (50%).
- **Toolbar Wrap Prevention:** Single-row action toolbar with nowrap flex layout; Reset button remains strictly on the top line.
- **Decoupled Feed Status:** Instant \`LIVE\` operational status upon table detection without getting stuck on \`WAITING\`.
- **Real-Time Lead Inventory Engine:** Proactive tracking of New Leads Added (+delta), Large Lead Reductions ($\ge 500$ or $\ge 10\%$), Out of Leads alerts, and campaign lifecycle additions/removals.
- **Persistent Action Center Alerts:** Sales, Goals, and Lead inventory alerts persist across refreshes until individually acknowledged via \`[NOTED]\`.
- **Strict Observer Safety:** Zero network requests, zero simulated button clicks, zero DOM modifications to native CallTools tables.
